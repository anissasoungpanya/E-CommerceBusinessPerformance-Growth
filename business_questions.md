1. Revenue & Growth
   - How much total revenue does the business produce?
   - How does revenue change and perform month by month?
   - Is revenue growth due to a high number of orders or orders of high value?
   - What is the average order value?
   - What are the sale trends and patterns presented, if any?
     
2. Product Performance
   - Which product categories generate the most revenue?
   - Which product categories generate the most orders?
   - Which categories are growing or declining?
   - Which products have the highest average selling price?
   - Are categories generating high revenue but low customer satisfaction?

3. Customer Analytics
   - How many unique customer purchases are there?
   - What percentage of customers are returning customers?
   - How much revenue comes from returning customers?
   - How much does an average customer spend?
   - How much time spans between a customer making another purchase?
   - Are customers purchasing the same categories repeatedly, or across different categories?
     
5. Geographic Performance
   - Which states generate the most revenue?
   - Where are the most customers located?
   - Which geographic regions have the highest average order value?
   - Do some regions experience slower delivery times?
     
7. Operations & Customer Experience
   - How long does it take orders to be delivered on average?
   - What percentage of orders are delivered late and on time?
   - Do late deliveries result in lower review scores?
   - Which regions or sells have the worst delivery performance?
