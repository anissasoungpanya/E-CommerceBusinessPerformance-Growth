# E-CommerceBusinessPerformance-Growth

Problem
An e-commerce business would like to understand what is driving their revenue and where they should focus their efforts to improve revenue growth. 

Analyis
In order to investigate this problem, I split the analysis into 5 categories:

1) Revenue and Business Performance
     
2) Product and Category Performance
    
3) Customer Behavior and Retention

4) Geographic and Market Performance

5) Operations and Customer Experience

Data
The data used for this project is Kaggle's Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist. This dataset contains information of 100k orders from 2016-2018 made at multiple marketplaces at Brazil.
